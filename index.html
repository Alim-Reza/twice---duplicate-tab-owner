Twice – Duplicate Tab Blocker

Privacy Policy · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short: Twice does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any data. It has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything it does happens inside your own browser.

What the extension accesses, and why

To notice that you are opening a page that is already open, Twice compares the address (URL) of a new tab with the addresses of the tabs you already have open. This comparison happens entirely on your device, in the moment. Addresses are never written to a log, kept as browsing history, or sent anywhere.

To ask "You already have this tab open. Open it again?", Twice briefly shows a small dialog on top of the page you already had open. It does not read or change the content of the page.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings (whether Twice is on, and the list of sites you told it to ignore) are kept in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device.
  • Temporary state: the question currently on screen, and the list of tabs you closed with "Scan & close duplicates" so that Undo works for 10 minutes. This is kept in chrome.storage.session, which lives in memory and is cleared when the browser closes.

Nothing is synced to your Google account, and nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

Network access

Twice makes no network requests of its own: it does not load remote code, contact servers, or "phone home".

Permissions

  • tabs: to read the addresses of open tabs and switch to the one you already have.
  • webNavigation: to notice when a new tab finishes going to a page, including after redirects.
  • scripting and access to all sites: to show the "already open" dialog on whichever page you already have open. The dialog can appear on any site, so the permission covers all sites.
  • storage: to remember your settings on your device.

Children

Twice collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Any change that would involve collecting data would require your consent first.
